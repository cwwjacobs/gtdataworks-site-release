How engagements run

The same five steps for a $500 prompt pack and a $10,000 assistant. The evaluation criteria are agreed before the build starts, so “done” is not a matter of opinion.

  1. You send a brief

    Use the brief builder on the contact page, or email agents@gtdataworks.com. Say what the agent should do, which systems it touches, and roughly when you need it.

  2. A scoping call

    A call to walk through the systems involved, what a good result looks like, and what is out of scope.

  3. A fixed quote

    A written quote with the price, the deliverables, the timeline, and the evaluation criteria the work will be graded against, agreed up front. Retainer and hourly work gets a rate and an estimate instead of a fixed price.

  4. The build

    Work ships in milestones, each with its receipts: test results, action logs and hashes of what was delivered. You get a short written note every week on what moved and what comes next.

  5. Handover

    Source code, documentation, the evaluation results and a recorded walkthrough, so your team can run and change the work without GTDataWorks.

Pricing notes

Prices are in USD.

Tiers are starting points. The price shown is where a scoped engagement of that kind begins; the final quote follows the scoping call.

Fixed tiers cover scoped builds, audits and evaluations. Hourly rates cover retainers, ongoing iteration, deployment, hardening and consulting: $50–$100/hr for retainers and reporting, $80–$150/hr for development, deployment and hardening, $100–$200/hr for consulting.

Managed MCP servers are priced as a setup fee plus monthly hosting per server; a per-call model is available for high-volume servers.

Receipts are listed among each service's deliverables. They are not priced separately.
