Assistants that live inside HubSpot, Salesforce or your ERP.

What it is

Your sales, finance or HR team already lives in HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or an ERP. Rather than adding another tab, GTDataWorks puts an assistant inside that system through its API. It summarizes an account before a call, drafts the follow-up after it, generates a report from live records, schedules the tasks that fall out of a meeting, and surfaces what changed since last week.

To your team it looks like a note, a task or a draft that appears where they already work. Behind it, every read is scoped to the objects you agreed, and every write back to the CRM is logged with what the agent saw and why it acted, so an admin can review any change and see the reasoning behind it.

What you get

The field-mapping document is the contract: what the assistant may read, what it may write, and under which rules. Everything else is built to it, and the receipt log shows it was followed.

  • Integration code or workflow
  • Field-mapping document
  • Receipt log
  • User guide

How the work runs

  1. Map the objects

    A working session with your admin and one person from the team: which objects the assistant reads (accounts, deals, tickets, invoices), which fields it may write, and who may trigger it. The result is the field-mapping document.

  2. Connect read-only

    An API connection through n8n, Zapier or code, using a service account you create and scoped to read. The assistant starts by summarizing accounts and drafting follow-ups it does not send.

  3. Add write-back behind rules

    Each write (a note, a task, a status change, a saved draft) is checked against the rules in the mapping and logged as an action receipt: what the agent saw, what it decided and why.

  4. Test against a sandbox

    Scripted cases run against your CRM's sandbox or a copy of the data, then a handful of live records. You review the receipt log with the studio before anything touches production.

  5. Roll out and hand over

    Live for one team first, then wider. You receive the integration code or workflow, the field-mapping document, the receipt log and a user guide written for the people who will use it, not for the people who built it.

Where it fits

A good fit when

  • A sales, finance or HR team works in HubSpot, Salesforce, Zoho or an ERP with a REST API.
  • The work recurs inside the CRM: account summaries, follow-ups, reports from live records, task scheduling.
  • An admin wants every automated write to be reviewable after the fact.

Not a fit when

  • The CRM or ERP has no API. There is nothing to integrate against; AI Consulting & Proof-of-Concept can help find another route.
  • You need Salesforce Apex, SAP-native or Dynamics-native development. GTDataWorks integrates through REST APIs, n8n and Zapier.
  • The work spans several systems with branching decisions: that is Agent Orchestration & Workflow Automation. An LLM feature inside your own product is AI Integration & APIs.

Questions

Where does the CRM data live during and after the project?

In your CRM. The assistant reads and writes through the API with a service account you create, and any n8n or Zapier workflow runs in your workspace. Records the assistant summarizes are sent to the model provider you choose, under your own API key and its data terms. The studio keeps no copy of your records.

Who approves what the assistant writes?

The write-back rules in the field-mapping document decide. Common settings: drafts are saved but never sent, notes and tasks are written directly, and status or amount changes wait for a person to confirm. Every write, confirmed or automatic, is logged as an action receipt.

Which CRMs and ERPs?

HubSpot, Salesforce and Zoho through their REST APIs, and any ERP with a documented API. The work is done through the API, n8n and Zapier. The studio does not write Salesforce Apex or SAP-native code, and will say so at scoping if your case needs it.

What happens after handover?

Your team keeps the user guide; your admin keeps the field-mapping document and the receipt log, which keeps growing as the assistant works. New objects or changed rules are scoped as further work.
